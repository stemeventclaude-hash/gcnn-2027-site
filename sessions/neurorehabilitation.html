Session overview

Recovery after stroke, brain injury and progressive disease is where most neurological care actually happens, and the evidence base has moved quickly. GCNN 2027 covers intensity and timing of therapy after stroke, vagus nerve stimulation paired with rehabilitation for upper-limb recovery, robotic and exoskeleton-assisted gait training, constraint-induced movement therapy, and the use of telerehabilitation to maintain dose between clinic visits. Sessions also address spasticity management, aphasia therapy and the outcome measures used to judge whether any of it worked.

Topics covered

  • Dose, intensity and timing of therapy after stroke
  • Paired VNS for upper-limb motor recovery
  • Robotic and exoskeleton-assisted gait training
  • Constraint-induced movement therapy and motor relearning
  • Telerehabilitation and remote dose maintenance
  • Spasticity management and botulinum toxin protocols
  • Aphasia and cognitive rehabilitation
  • Function-based outcome measures and minimal clinically important difference
