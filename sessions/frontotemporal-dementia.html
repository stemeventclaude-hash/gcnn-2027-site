Frontotemporal Dementia

8 focus areas · part of the Dementia, Cognitive & Behavioural Neurology track.

Submit an abstractAll tracks
02Scientific trackDementia, Cognitive & Behavioural NeurologySubmit abstractBack to track

Session overview

FTD management is advancing with progranulin-replacement therapy and emerging tau-targeted approaches. The session covers latozinemab (AL001) and the INFRONT-3 phase 3 trial, AL001 progranulin replacement mechanism, MAPT-targeted therapies in development, FTD-ALS spectrum and C9orf72 expansion, behavioural variant FTD (bvFTD) and primary progressive aphasia (PPA) subtypes, and the diagnostic criteria challenges. Discussion addresses NfL and other CSF biomarkers, FTD imaging biomarkers (FDG-PET, tau-PET), pre-symptomatic identification in familial FTD, caregiver burden in bvFTD specifically, the comparison with Alzheimer's disease management, and ALSFTD spectrum and the C9orf72 ASO programmes (BIIB078 lessons and newer-generation candidates).

Topics covered

  • Latozinemab INFRONT-3
  • Progranulin replacement mechanism
  • MAPT-targeted therapies
  • FTD-ALS spectrum (C9orf72)
  • bvFTD vs PPA subtypes
  • Pre-symptomatic familial FTD
  • Caregiver burden
  • C9orf72 ASO programmes
