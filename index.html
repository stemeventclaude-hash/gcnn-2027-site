About the congress June 22–23 · Prague

Two days where neurology moves forward.

GCNN 2027 convenes neurologists, neuroscientists, clinical researchers and healthcare professionals in Prague for two days of presentations and discussion.

  1. 01
    Evidence that moves practice

    Stroke, dementia, MS, Parkinson’s, epilepsy and neuromuscular disease, with work that changes care.

  2. 02
    Bench to bedside, in one room

    Clinicians, researchers and industry R&D sharing the same sessions and the same questions.

  3. 03
    Where the field is heading

    AI-assisted diagnosis, neuroimaging and brain-computer interfaces alongside clinical neurology.

Quick answers

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before you register. The secretariat replies to every enquiry within 24 hours.

All 13 FAQs
01Who should attend this conference?

The programme is built for university faculty, scientists, R&D and industry professionals, policy-makers, founders, and graduate / doctoral students looking to stay ahead of current research and emerging practice in the field.

02What is the process for completing my registration?

Open the Register page on this website, choose your participation mode and category, and submit the secure online form. Confirmation is instant and an official receipt follows within 2-4 business days. Early-bird pricing applies if you register before the published cut-off.

03Is there a limit on how many abstracts one author can submit?

Each author may submit up to two abstracts, in oral, poster, or a combination of both formats.

04Will the organisers issue official documentation to support my visa application?

Yes — but only for registered, paid-up participants. Once your payment is confirmed, a personalised invitation letter is issued within 3-5 business days, which you can submit alongside your visa application.

05What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Registration fees are generally non-refundable, and cancellation, transfer and refund requests are subject to published conditions and deadlines. For the complete cancellation and refund policy, please read our Terms & Conditions.
