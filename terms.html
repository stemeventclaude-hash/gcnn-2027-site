1. Definitions

“Organizer”, “we”, “us” and “our” means SCON Conferences. “Participant” or “you” means any presenter, author, speaker, delegate, attendee, purchaser or other person registering for, accepting an invitation to, accessing or attending an Event. “Event” means the applicable conference, academic meeting, paper presentation, workshop or related activity organized by the Organizer.

“Registration” means the accepted participation arrangement for an Event. “Registration Fee” means the fee stated on the applicable registration page, invoice or written confirmation.

2. Acceptance of Terms

These Terms apply to all Events organized by the Organizer unless an Event-specific written provision expressly provides otherwise.

By registering, submitting an abstract or paper, making payment, accepting an invitation, accessing an Event platform or attending an Event, you confirm acceptance of these Terms.

Electronic actions, including submitting an online registration, selecting an acceptance option or completing payment, may constitute acceptance where permitted by applicable law.

3. Event Information and Programme

We make every reasonable effort to provide accurate information about Event dates, venue, programme, speakers, presentation format and schedule.

International events involve external service providers and circumstances that may require reasonable changes because of speaker availability, venue requirements, travel disruption, safety considerations, government directions or other operational circumstances.

The Organizer may reasonably change the programme or session order; speakers, chairpersons or moderators; presentation dates or times; session duration or format; rooms or venue within the destination city; virtual-event platforms; or in-person, virtual or hybrid delivery arrangements.

Material changes will be communicated as reasonably practicable. A reasonable programme or schedule adjustment will not ordinarily be treated as cancellation. Participants should regularly check the latest programme and Event communications.

The inclusion of a particular speaker, chairperson, moderator, reviewer, sponsor, venue or partner does not constitute a guarantee that such person or organization will participate or remain available for the Event.

4. Registration and Confirmation

Registration is considered confirmed only after all required information has been provided, full cleared payment has been received where payment is required, the registration has been accepted and the Organizer has issued a confirmation or receipt.

Abstract acceptance, an invitation letter or submission of a registration form does not by itself confirm registration.

Participants must provide complete and accurate personal, professional and academic information.

Registrations are personal and must not be shared, resold or transferred without prior written approval.

The Organizer may decline or cancel a registration if information is materially false, incomplete or misleading; payment is reversed or disputed; these Terms are seriously violated; or participation presents a legal, safety, security or operational concern.

5. Registration Fees and Payment

Registration fees must be paid in the currency and by the deadline stated on the registration page or invoice.

Unless expressly included, the Participant is responsible for applicable bank charges, payment-processing charges, foreign-exchange costs and intermediary-bank deductions and must ensure that the full invoice amount is received.

Discounts, promotional prices and special offers cannot ordinarily be combined or applied retrospectively unless confirmed in writing.

Registration fees may cover participation, programme administration, venue arrangements, technology, catering, materials, certificates and other services described for the relevant category. No separate refund is available for an included item that a Participant chooses not to use.

6. Cancellation, Registration Transfer and Refund Policy

We understand that circumstances may change and participants may occasionally be unable to attend a conference as planned. The GCNN 2027 Secretariat aims to handle cancellation, registration transfer and refund requests fairly and transparently while accounting for the arrangements, services and expenses committed in preparation for each event.

Registration fees are generally non-refundable. However, eligible requests may be considered in accordance with the terms outlined below.

6.1 Submission of Requests

All cancellation, registration transfer and refund requests must be submitted in writing to the GCNN 2027 Secretariat at secretariat@neuroscience-conference.com. Verbal requests cannot be processed.

Requests must include the participant’s full name, registration or invoice number, payment details where applicable, and a clear description of the request.

Requests for cancellation or refund must generally be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the original payment date and, where a refund is requested, at least sixty (60) calendar days before the conference start date. Both conditions must be satisfied for a request to be considered under the standard refund procedure.

Requests received outside these timelines may be declined. However, the Secretariat may consider exceptional circumstances at its discretion, subject to applicable laws and the terms communicated at registration.

6.2 What Your Registration Covers

Once the full payment is received and registration is confirmed, the conference team begins preparing and arranging services for the participant. Depending on the selected registration package, these may include:

  • Official Conference Website Profile: Your professional profile will be published on the official conference website, providing visibility to the global academic and research community from the time of registration confirmation until the conclusion of the conference.
  • Conference ID Card and Certificate of Participation: Your official delegate ID card (badge) and Certificate of Participation will be prepared in advance upon confirmation of your registration.
  • Oral Presentation Opportunity: You will be allocated a dedicated presentation slot in the official scientific programme to present your research, share your findings and engage with fellow participants.
  • Access to Scientific Sessions: You will have access to all scheduled conference sessions, including keynote lectures, invited talks, scientific presentations and panel discussions.
  • Conference Materials: You will receive access to the official scientific programme and other relevant conference materials to support your participation.
  • Abstract Publication: Your accepted research abstract will be published in the official conference proceedings (e-abstract book), providing an opportunity to showcase your work to the wider academic community.
  • Meals and Refreshments: You will be provided with lunch, scheduled coffee breaks and refreshments during the conference, as specified in your registration package.
  • Academic Networking Opportunities: Connect and interact with international researchers, academicians, clinicians and industry professionals, fostering knowledge exchange and potential research collaborations.
  • Marketing and Social Media Recognition: Your participation will be highlighted through the conference’s official promotional channels and social media platforms, enhancing your professional visibility.
  • Hotel Coordination: Your accommodation and conference participation details will be coordinated with the designated hotel, in accordance with the inclusions of your selected registration package (if applicable).
  • Past Editions and Speaker Archive: Your speaker profile, presentation title and participation details will be retained on the official conference website under the respective past edition, providing continued online visibility and a lasting record of your academic contribution beyond the conference dates.

These services and arrangements may involve costs or commitments incurred before the conference. Any refund assessment will take into account the services already provided, prepared or committed, together with applicable non-recoverable expenses.

6.3 Cancellation, Refund and Registration Transfer Requests

(a) Requests received more than 60 days before the conference

Participants who submit a written request more than sixty (60) calendar days before the conference start date and within thirty (30) calendar days of the original payment date may be considered for one of the following options:

  • Substitute participant: Transfer the registration to a colleague, subject to approval.
  • Registration transfer: Transfer the registration to another eligible edition of the conference, subject to availability and approval.
  • Registration credit: Receive a credit toward a future eligible conference, subject to the applicable terms.
  • Partial refund: Receive a partial refund in exceptional circumstances, subject to review and approval by the Secretariat.

Where a partial refund is approved, the amount will be calculated by deducting the value of services already provided, prepared or committed on the participant’s behalf, applicable administrative processing charges, non-recoverable expenses, and taxes or bank and payment-provider charges that cannot be recovered.

Any remaining amount attributable to services that have not been provided or committed may be refunded, subject to the Secretariat’s assessment and applicable laws.

The Secretariat will communicate the proposed refund calculation and outcome to the participant in writing.

(b) Requests received within 60 days of the conference

Cancellation and monetary refund requests submitted within sixty (60) calendar days of the conference start date will generally not be eligible for a refund.

  • However, participants may request a substitute participant, registration transfer or registration credit, subject to written approval and availability.

Any applicable price differences, administrative charges, accommodation costs or other conditions will be communicated before the transfer or credit is confirmed.

(c) Requests received on or after the conference start date

Requests submitted on the day the conference begins, after the conference has commenced, or after the participant’s scheduled participation has ended will generally not be eligible for cancellation, transfer or refund, except where required by applicable law or expressly approved by the Secretariat.

(d) Registration transfers

Participants who are unable to attend may request to transfer their registration to a colleague or, subject to approval, to a future edition of the GCNN 2027.

All transfer requests must be submitted in writing before the conference start date. Transfers will not be accepted on the day the conference begins or after the conference has commenced.

All transfers require written confirmation and approval from the Secretariat. The proposed substitute participant must satisfy the applicable registration requirements, and any differences in registration fees, package inclusions, accommodation arrangements or administrative charges must be settled as communicated by the Secretariat.

A transfer is not considered confirmed until written approval has been issued.

6.4 Participant Inability to Attend

Participants who cannot attend due to personal or professional commitments, illness, visa refusal or delay, travel disruption, lack of institutional approval or funding, scheduling conflicts, or other individual circumstances must submit their requests within the applicable timelines.

Such circumstances do not automatically entitle a participant to cancellation, transfer or monetary refund. Each request will be assessed according to the eligibility criteria, services already provided or committed, and applicable terms.

Non-attendance without prior notice, late arrival, early departure, failure to join a virtual session, or a participant’s decision not to use an included service will generally not qualify for a refund.

Participants are encouraged to arrange appropriate travel, medical and cancellation insurance where relevant.

6.5 Conference Rescheduling or Postponement

If the GCNN 2027 is postponed or rescheduled, registered participants will be notified of the revised dates and arrangements through the contact details provided during registration.

Participants who are unable to attend on the revised dates may submit a written request for one of the following options:

  • Registration transfer: Transfer the registration to a colleague or an eligible future edition, subject to written approval.
  • Registration credit: Apply the eligible registration amount toward a future conference, subject to the applicable terms.
  • Partial refund: Request a partial refund, subject to review, applicable administrative charges, the value of services already provided or committed, and non-recoverable expenses.

The Secretariat will communicate the available options and the deadline for submitting requests following the postponement or rescheduling announcement.

All requests must be submitted within the timeframe specified in that communication. The Secretariat will review each request and communicate the applicable options, conditions and deductions in writing.

A rescheduling does not automatically entitle participants to a full refund. Refund eligibility will be assessed in accordance with this policy and applicable laws.

6.6 Discounted and Special-Offer Registrations

Registrations purchased under early-bird offers, promotional campaigns, special discounts, bundled packages or other preferential pricing arrangements are subject to the applicable cancellation, transfer and refund conditions outlined in this policy.

Refunds for discounted registrations will be assessed individually based on the amount actually paid, the terms of the offer, services provided or committed, and applicable non-recoverable expenses and charges.

Where a discounted registration cannot be refunded, the Secretariat may offer a registration transfer, future-event credit or virtual participation option, if available and appropriate to the circumstances.

If an in-person conference is rescheduled and virtual participation is offered, participants holding special discounted registrations may be offered virtual attendance as an alternative. The applicable options and conditions will be communicated in writing.

No alternative arrangement or refund is guaranteed unless confirmed in writing by the Secretariat, subject to applicable laws.

6.7 Accommodation and Hotel Reservations

Where accommodation is included in or purchased as part of a registration package, hotel reservations are subject to the applicable hotel’s cancellation, amendment and refund terms.

Participants requesting cancellation or transfer of accommodation must notify the Secretariat in writing as early as possible.

Where the hotel permits cancellation and the relevant amount is recoverable, the eligible accommodation amount may be refunded, subject to applicable administrative charges and other deductions under this policy.

Once the hotel’s cancellation deadline has passed, or where the hotel retains payment for reserved rooms, the non-recoverable accommodation charge may be deducted from any approved refund.

Any accommodation refund or adjustment will depend on the hotel’s applicable terms and the actual amount recoverable by the organizer.

6.8 Circumstances Beyond the Organizers’ Reasonable Control

If the GCNN 2027 is disrupted, postponed, cancelled or otherwise affected by circumstances beyond the organizers’ reasonable control, including government restrictions, public emergencies, venue unavailability or other unforeseen events, the Secretariat will communicate the available arrangements to registered participants.

Depending on the circumstances, these arrangements may include rescheduling the conference, transferring registrations to a future edition, offering virtual participation or providing another appropriate alternative.

Any refund eligibility will be assessed in light of the circumstances, services provided or committed, applicable non-recoverable expenses, the terms communicated to participants and applicable laws.

The Secretariat will communicate the available options and applicable conditions in writing.

6.9 Refund Processing and Communication

All cancellation and refund requests are subject to review and written approval by the GCNN 2027 Secretariat.

The Secretariat will acknowledge receipt of each request within three (3) business days and aim to provide a substantive response within fifteen (15) business days. Where additional information or third-party confirmation is required, the participant will be informed of any resulting delay.

Approved refunds will generally be processed either through the original payment method or by bank account transfer, depending on the mutually agreed or applicable convenient method. The expected processing timeframe will be communicated to the participant in writing after the refund has been approved and the applicable refund amount has been determined.

The final refund amount may reflect approved deductions for services already provided or committed, administrative processing charges, non-recoverable expenses, and applicable taxes or bank and payment-provider charges that cannot be recovered.

All approved refunds, deductions, registration credits and transfer arrangements will be confirmed in writing.

6.10 Contact Information

For cancellation, registration transfer, refund or accommodation-related requests, please contact:

GCNN 2027 Secretariat
Email: secretariat@neuroscience-conference.com

Please include your full name, registration or invoice number, and a clear explanation of your request to help us process it efficiently.

This policy is subject to applicable laws. Where any provision conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the applicable legal requirement will prevail.

7. Cancellation, Postponement, Relocation or Format Change by the Organizer

The Organizer will make every reasonable effort to conduct each Event as announced. Circumstances may require cancellation, postponement, relocation or a change in delivery format.

If the Organizer cancels an Event for reasons within its reasonable control and does not offer a replacement date or substantially equivalent format, Participants will be offered an appropriate remedy, which may include a refund of the registration fee received for that Event.

If an Event is postponed, rescheduled, relocated or changed between in-person, virtual and hybrid formats, the existing registration will normally remain valid. If the change is material, the Organizer may offer a transfer, credit or another reasonable remedy.

The Organizer’s responsibility will ordinarily be limited to the registration fee actually received. We cannot reimburse independently booked flights, hotels, visas, taxis, insurance, loss of salary or business, or expenses paid directly to third parties.

8. Circumstances Beyond Reasonable Control

An Event may be affected by circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the Organizer, including natural disasters, extreme weather, epidemic or pandemic, war, terrorism, civil disturbance, strikes, government restrictions, border closures, transportation disruption, venue failure, utility or internet outages, cyber incidents, public-health measures or other emergencies.

The Organizer will make reasonable efforts to protect Participants’ interests and preserve the Event through rescheduling, relocation, format changes, virtual participation or registration credit. Any refund will depend on the circumstances, applicable law and the extent to which committed expenses can be recovered.

9. Visas, Immigration and Travel

The Organizer may provide reasonable visa-support documentation to eligible Participants with confirmed registration. The Organizer does not control decisions or processing times of embassies, consulates, immigration departments or border authorities.

An invitation or participation letter confirms the stated purpose of participation. It is not immigration or legal advice, financial sponsorship, employment authorization or a guarantee that a visa or entry permission will be issued.

Participants are responsible for maintaining a valid passport; checking visa, transit and entry requirements; applying sufficiently in advance; providing truthful information; attending required interviews; carrying supporting documents; and complying with immigration and local laws.

Visa refusal, delay or inability to enter the destination country does not automatically create a right to a refund. The cancellation provisions of these Terms apply.

The Organizer may verify or withdraw an invitation where documents or registration information appear altered, misrepresented or misused.

10. Academic Submissions and Presentations

Authors confirm that submitted materials are original or appropriately licensed, correctly identify contributors, contain appropriate citations, respect intellectual-property and privacy rights, comply with research and ethical standards and are not unlawful or deliberately misleading.

Submissions may be screened for plagiarism, duplication, manipulated content, fabricated authorship or other integrity concerns. The Organizer may request clarification or corrections and may withdraw acceptance if a serious concern is identified before or after the Event.

Abstract acceptance does not automatically guarantee publication, independent journal acceptance, indexing, citation, an award, a particular presentation duration or participation by every advertised reviewer or speaker. Any publication or indexing arrangement must be expressly confirmed in writing.

Presenters must follow applicable registration deadlines, assigned schedules and required presentation formats. Certificates may be issued only after applicable attendance, presentation, identity and payment requirements are completed.

11. Intellectual Property

Participants retain ownership of their original academic work.

By submitting or presenting material, the Participant grants the Organizer a non-exclusive, worldwide and royalty-free permission to host, reproduce, display, stream, record and distribute the submitted title, abstract, biography, presentation and related Event recording for Event delivery, proceedings, archives, documentation and reasonable promotion.

Participants should notify the Organizer before the Event if specific unpublished or confidential material must not be recorded or distributed. The Organizer will make reasonable efforts to accommodate the request, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed for material presented during an open session.

Participants must not record, reproduce, livestream or distribute another person’s presentation without permission.

12. Photography, Audio and Video Recording

Photography, audio recording, video recording or livestreaming may take place in public Event areas and during sessions. Participants may appear incidentally in Event photographs or recordings used for documentation, archives, reporting and reasonable promotion.

A Participant who does not wish to appear prominently in promotional material should contact secretariat@neuroscience-conference.com before the Event. The Organizer will make reasonable efforts to respect the request, although exclusion from background or crowd images cannot always be guaranteed.

13. Virtual and Hybrid Participation

Participants are responsible for a compatible device, stable internet connection, supported software, functional audio and video equipment and an appropriate participation environment.

The Organizer will make reasonable efforts to provide reliable access and technical instructions. Uninterrupted access cannot be guaranteed where a problem arises from Participant equipment, local internet, a third-party platform, telecommunications networks or circumstances outside our control.

Access links and credentials are personal and must not be shared. Participants must not record sessions, share restricted content, disrupt proceedings or attempt unauthorized access to Event systems.

14. Professional Conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully with staff and other participants, follow programme and reporting times, observe venue/platform/safety/security instructions, respect cultural and professional differences, protect privacy and intellectual property, and raise concerns through designated channels.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, abusive communication, stalking, deliberate disruption, physical aggression, impersonation or unlawful conduct will not be accepted.

Serious or repeated violations may result in restricted participation or removal without refund and, where necessary, referral to the venue, institution, platform or appropriate authorities.

Nothing in this section prevents a Participant from submitting a genuine complaint, seeking legal advice or exercising lawful consumer rights.

15. Safety, Health and Accessibility

Participants are responsible for evaluating their own health, mobility, travel and insurance needs before attending.

Participants must follow reasonable safety, identification, access-control and emergency instructions issued by the Organizer or venue.

Participants requiring accessibility support should contact secretariat@neuroscience-conference.com as early as possible so requirements can be discussed with the venue and reasonable arrangements considered.

The Organizer does not provide medical, travel, immigration or insurance advice.

16. Venues and Other Third-Party Services

International events depend on independent venues, hotels, airlines, publishers, payment processors, virtual platforms, caterers and other providers, which may apply their own terms.

Unless the Organizer expressly sells a service as part of the registration package, it is not responsible for independent bookings made directly between a Participant and a third party.

References to speakers, institutions, journals, sponsors, venues or partners should not be interpreted as a guarantee or assumption of responsibility by those parties unless expressly stated.

17. Event Outcomes and Limitation of Liability

The Organizer will make reasonable efforts to deliver a professional programme and valuable opportunities for learning, presentation and networking. Individual outcomes depend on factors outside the Organizer’s control.

The Organizer cannot guarantee publication or indexing; academic or professional recognition; employment or business opportunities; immigration outcomes; collaboration with a particular person or institution; financial returns; or any specific personal or institutional benefit.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Organizer will not be responsible for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of income, opportunity, reputation, data, travel arrangements or anticipated publication.

Where liability cannot legally be excluded, the Organizer’s aggregate contractual liability will ordinarily be limited to the registration fee actually received from the affected Participant for the relevant Event. Nothing in these Terms limits a right or liability that cannot legally be excluded.

18. Questions, Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Participants should write to secretariat@neuroscience-conference.com with their full name, registration or invoice number, Event name, clear explanation, supporting documents and requested resolution.

The Organizer will ordinarily acknowledge a complaint within three business days and aim to provide a substantive response within 15 business days. Complex matters may require additional time.

If a chargeback is raised, the Organizer may provide the payment provider with relevant registration, attendance, service-delivery and communication records. This does not restrict any lawful consumer, banking or payment-dispute right.

These Terms and the relationship between the Participant and the Organizer are governed by the laws of India.

If a disagreement arises, both parties agree to first make a genuine effort to resolve it through respectful written communication. If unresolved, the parties may consider mediation or another mutually acceptable resolution process.

Subject to applicable consumer-protection laws and other mandatory legal rights, the courts at Telangana, India will have jurisdiction.

Event-specific terms stated on a registration page, invoice or written confirmation will supplement these Terms. If an Event-specific provision directly conflicts with a general provision, the clearly identified Event-specific provision will apply.

These Terms, together with applicable Event-specific terms and any separate written agreement signed by the parties, constitute the terms governing participation in the Event.

If any part of these Terms is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue to apply. A delay in enforcing a provision does not permanently waive it.
