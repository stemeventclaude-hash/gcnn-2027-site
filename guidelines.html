Your abstract

What every submission must contain, and the shape to write it in.

01General abstract submission requirements

All abstracts must comply with the following requirements:

  1. Relevance: the abstract must align with the conference theme or one of its designated scientific topics.
  2. Scientific content: clearly describe the research objectives, methodology, principal findings and conclusions, as applicable.
  3. Abstract title: clear, concise, informative, and limited to 25 words.
  4. Word limit: 250–400 words, excluding the title, author details, affiliations and keywords, unless otherwise specified for the particular conference.
  5. Language: abstracts must be submitted in English.
  6. Author information: full names of all authors, with institutional affiliations, departments, cities and countries.
  7. Corresponding author: identify one corresponding author and provide a valid email address.
  8. Presenting author: clearly identify the author who will deliver the presentation.
  9. Keywords: include three to five relevant keywords.
  10. Scientific accuracy: all information, data, references and conclusions must be accurate and appropriately attributed.
  11. Ethical compliance: research involving human participants, animals, confidential information or identifiable patient data must comply with applicable ethical, institutional and legal requirements.
  12. Scientific track: select the most relevant topic or scientific track during submission, where applicable.

Authors are responsible for verifying the accuracy of their abstracts and obtaining approval from all co-authors before submission.

02Recommended abstract structure

Authors are encouraged to organise their abstracts using the following structure, where applicable:

  • Title — a concise and informative title representing the research.
  • Background or introduction — the scientific context and the problem being addressed.
  • Objectives — the principal research aim or question.
  • Methods — study design, methodology, data collection and analytical approach.
  • Results — the principal findings, including relevant quantitative data where available.
  • Conclusion — the significance of the findings and their implications.
  • Keywords — three to five relevant terms.

Alternative structures may be used for case reports, systematic reviews, theoretical studies and other specialised research formats.

Authors should avoid unsupported claims, unnecessary abbreviations, excessive promotional language, and references that are not essential to understanding the abstract.

How you present

Oral, virtual and encore formats — each subject to scientific review.

01Oral presentation guidelines

Authors may request an oral presentation during abstract submission, where this option is available. Oral presentations are subject to scientific review and acceptance.

Presentation format

  • Prepare the presentation in Microsoft PowerPoint (.ppt or .pptx) or PDF format.
  • A widescreen 16:9 slide layout is recommended.
  • Use clear, professional, readable fonts, with consistent formatting throughout.
  • Slides should have concise text, appropriate headings, and clearly labelled figures, tables, charts and images.
  • Avoid overcrowded slides, excessive animations and unnecessary visual effects.
  • All data, images, figures and references must be accurate and appropriately credited.
  • The presentation title should correspond to the accepted abstract.
  • The opening slide should include the presentation title, presenting author’s name and relevant institutional affiliation.
  • Patient-identifiable information and confidential research data must not be included without appropriate authorisation.
  • Presentations must be scientifically focused and must not contain unauthorised commercial advertising or misleading promotional claims.

Presentation content

An oral presentation should generally follow this sequence, where applicable:

  1. Title and author information.
  2. Background and research objectives.
  3. Materials and methods, or research methodology.
  4. Results and key findings.
  5. Discussion and scientific significance.
  6. Conclusion and implications.
  7. Relevant references and acknowledgements, where appropriate.

The content should be consistent with the accepted abstract. Any substantial changes to the research findings or conclusions should be communicated to the conference organisers where necessary.

Duration and file submission

  • The allotted presentation duration and question-and-answer period will be communicated in the acceptance notification or presentation instructions.
  • Presenters must adhere strictly to the assigned time limit.
  • Accepted presenters may be required to submit their presentation files before the deadline specified by the organisers.
  • Files must follow the prescribed format, naming conventions and size limits communicated by the conference.
  • Presenters should retain a backup copy of their presentation.
  • The scientific committee reserves the right to recommend an alternative presentation format based on the conference programme.

Acceptance of an abstract does not automatically guarantee an oral presentation slot.

02Virtual presentation guidelines

Virtual presentations are available to authors who wish to participate remotely, subject to the conference’s virtual participation arrangements. They may be delivered live or pre-recorded.

Live virtual presentation

  • The presenter delivers the presentation online at the scheduled session time.
  • Prepare slides in Microsoft PowerPoint (.ppt or .pptx) or PDF, as applicable.
  • A stable internet connection, functioning microphone, webcam where required, and a suitable presentation environment are necessary.
  • Slides must be clear, professional and consistent with the accepted abstract.
  • Join the virtual session in advance to check audio, video and screen-sharing arrangements.
  • Be available for questions and discussion if required by the session format.
  • Follow the technical instructions and presentation duration specified by the organisers.

Pre-recorded video presentation

Authors who prefer to record their presentations in advance may submit a pre-recorded video, subject to the conference’s instructions.

  • The video must be submitted before the deadline specified by the conference organisers.
  • MP4 is the recommended video format unless another format is specified.
  • The video must comply with the required duration, resolution, file size and submission instructions.
  • Audio must be clear, and slides or other visual materials must be legible.
  • The recording must accurately represent the accepted research and must not contain misleading claims.
  • The video should begin with the presentation title and identify the presenting author.
  • Authors must obtain the necessary permissions for any third-party images, figures, audio or copyrighted material included in the recording.
  • The organisers may require the presenter to attend a live question-and-answer session following the video presentation.

Technical and submission requirements

The organisers will communicate the accepted presentation format, technical specifications, submission deadline and any platform-specific instructions to accepted virtual presenters.

Presenters are responsible for ensuring that their presentation materials are compatible with the specified requirements. Failure to comply with the instructions may affect the presentation’s inclusion in the virtual programme.

03Encore abstract submission policy

An encore abstract describes research that has previously been presented at another scientific conference, academic meeting or professional event, or that has previously appeared in a publication. Such research may be considered for an encore presentation, provided the submission meets the following requirements.

Ownership and authorship

  • The submitted research must be the submitting author’s or authors’ own work, or research in which they are legitimate contributors.
  • Authors must not submit another researcher’s work as their own.
  • All original contributors must be appropriately credited.
  • The submitting author must have the necessary authority and rights to submit and present the work.
  • Where the work was previously published, authors must ensure that applicable copyright, licensing, publisher and original conference policies permit its reuse and presentation.

Disclosure of previous presentation or publication

Authors submitting an encore abstract must:

  1. Clearly disclose that the research or abstract has been presented or published previously.
  2. Provide the title and name of the previous conference, meeting, journal or publication, as applicable.
  3. Include the date of the previous presentation or publication and relevant citation details, where available.
  4. Disclose any previous publication in conference proceedings, journals or other publications.
  5. Identify significant updates, additional results or changes made to the research since its previous presentation or publication, where applicable.

Review and acceptance

  • Encore abstracts are subject to review and acceptance by the current conference’s scientific committee.
  • Previously published or presented work is not automatically guaranteed acceptance.
  • The committee may consider the relevance of the research, its scientific value, its suitability for the intended audience, and compliance with applicable publication and intellectual property requirements.
  • Authors must not falsely represent previously published or presented research as entirely new or previously unpublished work.
  • Submissions involving plagiarism, unauthorised reuse, misrepresentation of authorship or violations of publication rights may be rejected.
ImportantPreviously published research may be submitted as an encore abstract only when it is the authors’ own work, the original contributors are appropriately acknowledged, and the authors have the necessary rights and permissions for its submission and presentation.

Policies and review

Ethics, integrity, publication and the committee’s decisions.

01Previously published research and duplicate submission

The conference recognises that scientific research may be shared across multiple academic and professional forums. Previously published research may therefore be considered under the encore abstract policy, subject to the following conditions:

  • The research must be the submitting authors’ own work, or work in which they are legitimate contributors.
  • Prior publication or presentation must be disclosed transparently.
  • All original authors and contributors must be appropriately credited.
  • The authors must have the required rights and permissions to reuse and present the work.
  • The submission must comply with applicable journal, publisher, institutional and original conference policies.
  • Authors must not submit substantially identical abstracts multiple times to obtain additional presentation opportunities.
  • Any substantial updates or new findings must be clearly distinguished from previously published or presented material.
  • Authors must not submit work in a manner that violates publication agreements, exclusivity requirements or applicable copyright restrictions.

The scientific committee reserves the right to request clarification, supporting documentation or evidence of permission where necessary.

02Research ethics, authorship and disclosure

All authors are responsible for maintaining appropriate scientific and ethical standards.

  • Obtain ethics committee or institutional approval where required.
  • Obtain informed consent where applicable.
  • Protect confidential information and research participant privacy.
  • Disclose relevant funding sources and conflicts of interest.
  • Obtain approval from all listed co-authors before submission.
  • Secure permission to use copyrighted material where required.
  • Ensure that research findings are accurately represented.
  • Do not submit fabricated, falsified, plagiarised or knowingly misleading research.
  • Ensure that authorship information accurately reflects the contributions made to the research.

The conference scientific committee may request additional information or supporting documentation when necessary.

03Scientific integrity and promotional content

All abstracts and presentations must focus on scientific, academic, clinical or research-related content relevant to the conference. Commercial advertising, product promotion, unsupported claims and sales-oriented presentations are not permitted within scientific sessions unless expressly authorised as part of a designated sponsored session.

The organisers may screen submissions for plagiarism, duplicate submissions, fabricated or falsified data, misrepresented authorship and other research-integrity concerns. The organisers and scientific committee reserve the right to request corrections, reject a submission, withdraw acceptance, or remove a presentation from the programme if a serious violation of scientific integrity or conference policy is identified.

04Abstract review and acceptance

All submissions are evaluated through the conference’s scientific review process. Review criteria may include:

  • Relevance to the conference theme.
  • Scientific quality and methodological soundness.
  • Clarity, organisation and completeness.
  • Significance and potential contribution to the field.
  • Ethical compliance and appropriate disclosure of previous publication or presentation.
  • Suitability for the requested presentation format.

The scientific committee may accept an abstract, request revisions, recommend an alternative presentation format, or reject a submission. The corresponding author will be notified of the decision using the contact details provided during submission.

Acceptance of an abstract does not guarantee publication in a journal, indexing in an external database, or inclusion in a publication with a specific indexing status. Any publication or indexing arrangements will be communicated separately and must not be assumed unless expressly confirmed.

05Publication and conference proceedings

Accepted abstracts may be included in the official conference abstract book or proceedings where publication is offered for the relevant conference. Authors should note that:

  • Acceptance does not automatically guarantee journal publication, indexing or inclusion in an external database.
  • Publication arrangements, identifiers and indexing claims will be communicated separately, and will apply only where expressly confirmed.
  • Authors must ensure that they have the necessary rights and permissions for publication or republication of previously published work.
  • Encore abstracts must be appropriately disclosed and must not be misrepresented as new or unpublished research.
  • The organisers may exclude material from publication where required by applicable copyright, publication, ethical or scientific-integrity policies.

Authors are responsible for confirming that the proposed submission and publication do not conflict with any existing publication agreement.

06Abstract corrections and withdrawal

Authors who wish to correct or withdraw a submitted abstract must contact the conference secretariat in writing. The request should include:

  • Abstract title.
  • Submission reference number, if available.
  • Corresponding author’s name and contact details.
  • A description of the requested correction, or the reason for withdrawal.

Changes to the author list or other material details require approval from the organisers. Requests submitted after the conference programme or proceedings have been finalised may not be accommodated.

07Final policy

By submitting an abstract, authors confirm that the information provided is accurate, the work is appropriately attributed, and they have the authority to submit it for consideration.

The conference organisers and scientific committee reserve the right to review, accept, reject, request revisions to, or reclassify submissions in accordance with the conference’s scientific standards and applicable policies.

The organisers may update these guidelines when necessary. Conference-specific instructions published in the official call for abstracts, submission portal or acceptance notification take precedence where they differ from these general guidelines.
